# ☕ Monday Coffee – City Expansion Analysis

## 📌 Business Statement

Monday Coffee, launched in January 2023, has received a strong response from customers across several Indian cities through its online sales.

The company plans to open **three new coffee shops** and needs to identify the cities with the highest potential for expansion.

As a Data Analyst, the objective was to analyze **sales, customer, population, coffee consumption, and rental data** to recommend the top three cities.

## 🎯 Objectives

- ☕ Estimate coffee consumers in each city, assuming **25% of the population consumes coffee**.
- 💰 Calculate total coffee sales revenue for the last quarter of 2023.
- 📦 Analyze product-wise sales volume.
- 👥 Calculate average sales per customer by city.
- 🏙️ Compare city population with estimated coffee consumers.
- 🏆 Identify the top 3 selling products in each city.
- 👤 Analyze unique customers by city.
- 🏠 Compare average sales per customer with average rent per customer.
- 📈 Analyze monthly sales growth or decline.
- 🔎 Identify the top 3 cities based on sales, customers, rent, and estimated coffee consumers.

## 💡 Key Insights

- 🥇 **Delhi** has the highest estimated coffee consumers at **7.75M**, followed by **Mumbai (5.10M)** and **Pune (4.30M)**.
- 📉 **Lucknow (0.95M)** and **Jaipur (1.00M)** have the lowest estimated coffee consumers.
- 💰 **Pune** recorded the highest revenue at approximately **4.3M** during the last quarter of 2023.
- 👥 **Jaipur** has the highest number of unique customers (**69**) despite having a relatively smaller coffee-consuming population.
- 🏠 **Jaipur** has the lowest average rent per customer, while **Bangalore** has relatively high rent per customer.
- 🏆 Top 3 selling products:
  1. **Cold Brew Coffee Pack (6 Bottles)**
  2. **Ground Espresso Coffee (250g)**
  3. **Instant Coffee Powder (100g)**

## 🚀 Recommended Cities

### 🥇 Pune
- 💰 Revenue: **1,258,290**
- 📊 Average sale per consumer: **24,197**
- 🏠 Average rent: **294**
- 👥 Unique customers: **52**

### 🥈 Delhi
- 💰 Revenue: **750,420**
- 👥 Unique customers: **68**
- 🏠 Average rent: **330**
- ☕ Estimated coffee consumers: **7.75M**

### 🥉 Jaipur
- 💰 Revenue: **803,450**
- 🏠 Average rent: **156**
- 👥 Unique customers: **69**

## ✅ Conclusion

Based on the analysis of **sales, customers, coffee-consuming population, and rental costs**, **Pune, Delhi, and Jaipur** were identified as the three cities with strong potential for Monday Coffee's expansion.
